Identifier lookup is exact and works for any whole number. Name search only covers identities already discovered in this feed (up to the first 5,000); an infinite universe cannot be searched exhaustively.

    About the universe

    Every identity here is computed from its number. Identity 1 and identity 10100 are produced by the same rules, so nothing is stored and nothing runs out: the feed simply keeps generating the next batch as you scroll.

    Names, histories and portraits are fictional. The portraits are small procedurally drawn illustrations, not photographs of real people, and no hand-made artwork exists for each identity.

    The same number always gives the same identity, so a copied link or identifier will rebuild it on any device. Only your intro preference and last position are saved, in this browser.
